Frequently Asked Questions (Quick FAQs)❓

Got questions about bookings, payments, or custom trips? Find quick answers below!

1. How quickly will I get my booking confirmation ?
Once you submit your booking, our team verifies availability and sends your official hotel, flight, or cruise voucher to your Email and WhatsApp within 30 minutes to 2 hours..
2. Can I customize a holiday or cruise package ?
Yes! Whether you are traveling solo, as a couple, or with family, we can customize your hotel category, flight timings, sightseeing tours, and airport transfersto fit your budget.
3. What if I need to change my travel dates or cancel ?
We offer flexible rescheduling and cancellation options depending on your hotel or airline policy. Just message our 24/7 support team at least 24–48 hours before check-in, and we will handle the changes for you.
4. Do you assist with tourist visas and travel insurance ?
Yes, we provide complete visa documentation support and travel insurance for popular destinations including the UAE, Europe (Schengen), UK, and Asia.
5. What payment methods do you accept ?
We accept Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, credit/debit cards, and direct bank transfers. Partial advance booking options are also available on select holiday packages.
6. Who do I contact if I face an issue during my trip ?
Our 24/7 Trip Support Deskis always available via WhatsApp and phone to help with flight delays, hotel check-in assistance, or emergency itinerary changes while you are traveling.
