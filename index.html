Kerala

Kerala, famously known as "God’s Own Country," is one of India's most enchanting tourist destinations, celebrated for its lush landscapes, tranquil backwaters, and rich traditions. Travelers flock to Alleppey and Kumarakom to cruise along palm-fringed canals on traditional houseboats, while nature lovers escape to the misty tea plantations and wildlife sanctuaries of Munnar, Wayanad, and Thekkady. Along the Arabian Sea coast, pristine beaches like Kovalam and Varkala offer golden sands, dramatic cliffs, and rejuvenating Ayurvedic wellness retreats.

Top Destinations : Munnar • Alleppey • Wayanad • Fort Kochi • Kovalam • Varkala • Thekkady • Kumarakom

Ladakh

Ladakh, famously known as the "Land of High Passes," is one of India's most breathtaking tourist destinations, celebrated for its dramatic Himalayan landscapes, high-altitude lakes, and ancient Buddhist culture. Adventure seekers and nature lovers flock to Pangong Lake and Tso Moriri to witness their ever-changing shades of blue, while Nubra Valley captivates visitors with its surreal sand dunes and double-humped Bactrian camels. Spiritual heritage shines through centuries-old hilltop monasteries like Thiksey, Hemis, and Diskit, set against rugged mountain backdrops and fluttering prayer flags

Top Destinations: Leh • Pangong Lake • Nubra Valley • Khardung La • Tso Moriri • Thiksey Monastery • Magnetic Hill

Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu is one of India's premier tourist destinations, celebrated for its rich cultural heritage, grand architecture, and diverse natural beauty. The state is world-famous for its ancient Dravidian temples, particularly the colorful Meenakshi Amman Temple in Madurai and the UNESCO-listed Brihadeeswara Temple in Thanjavur. For nature lovers, misty hill stations like Ooty and Kodaikanal offer lush tea plantations, serene lakes, and cool weather. Along the coast, travelers can explore the historic rock-cut monuments of Mahabalipuram and leisure travelers alike.

Top Destinations: Kodaikanal • Madurai • Thanjavur • Mahabalipuram • Rameswaram • Kanyakumari • Chennai
